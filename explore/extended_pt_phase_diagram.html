Extended Pressure-Temperature Phase Diagram

June 2026

The Pressure vs Temperature phase diagram is one of the more popular physics-chemistry illustrations. Recently, while I was thinking about the melting point of silicon (which is very high), I went back to this diagram and after spending some time with it, I got the doubt of what would happen if we extend the quadrants.

So I went and explored this on Claude. This diagram extends the classical pressure-temperature chart into all four quadrants, including negative temperature (quantum population inversion) and negative pressure (tensile stress) regimes. Click on any region to explore its corresponding phase.

Rigorous extended pressure-temperature phase diagram across four quadrants. Q1 shows standard confirmed matter phases. Q2 shows population-inversion thermodynamics in bounded-spectrum systems. Q3 is marked theoretical and uncharted. Q4 shows confirmed condensed-matter behaviour under tensile stress with spinodal limit.

Click any region to explore
Hatched regions are speculative. The spinodal dashed curve in Q4 is a real, computable physical boundary. Q3 is marked theoretical because negative T and negative P are decoupled degrees of freedom with no established joint phase structure.
